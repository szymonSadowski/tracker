# 🧰 Best of AI Stack

## Prompt (verbatim)

Recommend the team's most effective AI stack right now, based on which models, MCP tools, agents, and skills correlate with the highest-quality sessions. Output shape: a 'More usage' list first (model, tools, agents, skills - one line each, a simple sentence saying who uses it and what quality it delivers), then a short 'Dropping off' list with plain-language reasons, then one line naming whose setup is most worth copying. Say small sample sizes in plain words ('based on only 3 scored sessions').
