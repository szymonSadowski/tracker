# 🚀 What can we do to go faster?

## Prompt (verbatim)

Find the biggest opportunities to speed the team up, based on the last 14 days' bottlenecks and the habits that separate top performers from the rest. Output shape: exactly 5 recommendations ranked by expected impact, one line each (action, expected impact, effort). No data-dump preface - the supporting evidence goes inline in each line.
