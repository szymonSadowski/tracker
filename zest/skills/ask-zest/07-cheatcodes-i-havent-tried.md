# 🔍 Cheatcodes I haven't tried

## Prompt (verbatim)

What practices, tools, and skills do my teammates use that I could try? Present each as a practice worth sharing, never as my gap or deficit - lead with what the teammate does and how it helps them. Output shape: one line on a practice of mine that teammates could borrow; then the top 3 teammate practices, one line each (who, what they use, how often, how to try it); end with a single friendly 'try this first' suggestion.
