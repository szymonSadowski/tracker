# 🛠️ Skills & Tools this week

## Prompt (verbatim)

Compare the team's tool, skill, and agent usage over the last 7 days against the previous week. Headline the net shift (new vs dropped). Cover only items with 2+ sessions or a clear standout: what's getting more usage and who uses it, what's dropping off, and one line on who is driving the change. Roll one-session noise into a single muted count.
