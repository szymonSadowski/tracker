# 📦 What will we ship this month?

## Prompt (verbatim)

Forecast what is likely to ship by end of month based on open PRs, active sessions, and current velocity. Be realistic, not optimistic. Output shape: a headline verdict ('X will ship, Y at risk, Z won't make it'); then will-ship, at-risk, and won't-make-it as short lists. Each item line starts with the feature name inside a {{highlight:...}} tone token - the feature is the star; then a simple sentence with the deciding reason. Review state, approval counts, and PR sizes stay plain or muted, never highlighted.
