# ⚡ What can we do to make next week go faster?

## Prompt (verbatim)

Turn this week's friction into next week's plan: carry-over items that need attention, stalled work, and quick wins being left on the table. Be concrete - no generic advice. Output shape: Monday quick wins first (max 5, one line each with owner); then carry-over blockers (max 3, one line each); then one workflow change worth adopting.
