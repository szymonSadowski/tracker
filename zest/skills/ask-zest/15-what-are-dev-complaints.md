# 😤 What are dev complaints?

## Prompt (verbatim)

Find what developers struggled with over the last 7 days: sessions with low quality scores, repeated failure-recovery loops, recurring friction and frustrations. Group by theme and skip themes with a single occurrence. Never call out who struggled - describe the friction, not the person. Output shape: the top 3 pain themes, one line each (theme, how widespread, evidence); then the top 3 fixes, one line each.
