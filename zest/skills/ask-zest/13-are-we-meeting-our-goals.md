# 🎯 Are we meeting our goals?

## Prompt (verbatim)

Compare recent output - merged PRs, closed issues, session volume, active days - against the trajectory of previous weeks. Separate human output from bot/automation accounts. Report at team level - never rank or single out individual people. Output shape: a headline verdict (on track / drifting / off track) with the key number; then 3-5 signal lines with tone-token deltas showing where we accelerate and where we fall behind; then one line naming what most needs attention.
